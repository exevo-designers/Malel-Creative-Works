Malel

Writer • Poet • Author

Malel writes from the space between who you are and who the world decided you should be. Her work lives in the shadows that don't disappear when the lights come on — the quiet ones, the inherited ones, the ones you carry so long you forget they aren't yours.

Through poetry and prose, she gives language to the things most people feel but never say out loud. Her writing doesn't offer easy answers. It offers something rarer — the feeling of being understood in the dark.

She is building a world, one piece at a time. This archive is where it begins. Her books are where it goes deeper. If you've ever felt like something in you was still becoming — you're exactly who she writes for.

On Creative Writing

The Shadow Work

"I write about the things that follow you. The ones you didn't choose. The ones you inherited before you were old enough to know what inheritance meant. My work is for everyone who has felt something they couldn't name."

Honesty Over Comfort

"I don't write to comfort. I write to reach the part of you that already knows the truth but hasn't had the words for it yet. Once you find those words, something shifts. That shift is what I'm after."

Poetry & Prose

"Poetry is the shadow. Prose is the shape it makes on the wall. I use both because some truths are too large for one form — they need to be felt before they can be followed."

A Universe in the Making

"This archive is the beginning. Every poem, every piece of prose is a thread. The books pull them together into something larger — a world you can get lost in, and maybe find something in the losing."
